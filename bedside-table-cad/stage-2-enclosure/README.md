# Stage 2: Enclosure

## Prerequisites
- Stage 1 complete (4 carcass panels exist)

## Goals
1. **Cut rabbets** in all 4 panels for the back panel (9mm deep × 6mm wide)
2. **Create back panel** and position in rabbets (432 × 561 × 6mm)
3. **Cut dados** in side panels and add false floor at Z=344mm

## Success Criteria
- [ ] Back panel sits flush with rear of cabinet
- [ ] False floor divides interior at correct height
- [ ] All joints are 6mm deep (enough for strength, not too deep)

## Files
- `rabbets_dados.py` - Cut locations for joinery
- `enclosure_parts.py` - Back panel and false floor specs
