# Dual-Tier Product Strategy - Low-Range & Mid-Range

**Strategy:** Two product tiers using same design, different materials  
**Purpose:** Capture multiple market segments, maximize market share  
**Date:** 2024-12-31

---

## 🎯 Strategy Overview

### Two Product Tiers

**Tier 1: Rustic Wedding Box - Standard** (Low-Range)
- **Price:** $65-75
- **Target:** Budget-conscious customers, high volume
- **Materials:** Pine or Poplar (cheaper wood)
- **Positioning:** Affordable rustic option

**Tier 2: Rustic Wedding Box - Premium** (Mid-Range)
- **Price:** $110-115
- **Target:** Quality-conscious customers, premium positioning
- **Materials:** Oak (premium wood)
- **Positioning:** Premium materials, better quality

## 🔧 Cost Reduction Strategies for Tier 1

### Option 1: Reduce Labor Costs

**Current:** 2.5 hours = $61.67  
**Target:** 1.5 hours = $37.00

**Strategies:**
- Batch production (5-10 boxes at once)
- Optimize CNC programs (faster cutting)
- Streamline assembly (jigs, fixtures)
- Reduce sanding time (better initial cuts)

**Potential Savings:** $24.67 per box

**New COGS:** $88.66 - $24.67 = **$63.99**

**Pricing at $70:**
- Profit: $6.01
- Margin: 8.6% ✅ (acceptable for volume play)

---

### Option 2: Use Cheaper Materials

**Current Materials:** $8.88  
**Target:** $5-6

**Changes:**
- Pine/Poplar: $0.38 (already cheap)
- Basic hinges: $1.50 each = $3.00
- Basic latch (no padlock): $1.50
- Felt instead of velvet: $0.75
- Basic finish: $0.50

**New Materials:** $6.13

**New Total COGS:** $6.13 + $61.67 + $18.11 = **$85.91**

**Still too high for $65-75 pricing**

### Option 3: Combination Approach ⭐ **RECOMMENDED**
