## 🪵 Wood Specifications

### Primary Material: Oak

**Type:** White Oak or Red Oak  
**Grade:** Select or Better (minimal knots, clear grain)  
**Moisture Content:** 6-8%  
**Thickness:** 0.5" (12.7 mm) - consistent across all panels  
**Grain:** Visible natural grain preferred for rustic aesthetic

**Board Requirements:**
- Width: Minimum 7" (for width panels)
- Length: Minimum 10" (for length panels)
- Thickness: 0.5" (planed to final thickness)

**Estimated Board Feet per Box:** 0.15 bf

## 🔩 Hardware Specifications

### Hinges

**Type:** Antique Brass Hinges  
**Quantity:** 2 per box  
**Size:** Small (approximately 1" x 0.75")  
**Style:** Recessed (hidden) or surface mount  
**Screws:** Brass screws, #4 or #6, 3/8" length  
**Installation:** 
- Back panel: Recessed mortise
- Lid back edge: Recessed mortise
- Position: Centered, 2" from each side

### Latch & Lock

**Latch Type:** Antique Brass Latch Mechanism  
**Components:**
- Latch plate (attached to lid front edge)
- Catch plate (attached to box front panel)
- Small padlock (antique brass finish)

**Padlock Specifications:**
- Size: Small (approximately 1.5" x 1")
- Type: Keyed padlock
- Finish: Antique brass
- Keys: 2 keys per lock

**Installation:**
- Latch plate: Center of lid front edge
- Catch plate: Center of box front panel
- Position: Top of front panel, centered horizontally

## 🎨 Finish Specifications

### Sanding

**Grit Progression:**
1. 120 grit - Rough sanding, remove saw marks
2. 180 grit - Medium sanding, smooth surfaces
3. 220 grit - Fine sanding, prepare for finish
4. 320 grit - Final sanding, ultra-smooth

**Edge Treatment:**
- All edges: Slightly rounded (1/32" radius / 0.8 mm)
- Safety: No sharp corners

### Finish Application

**Primary Finish:** Natural Oil  
**Options:**
1. **Linseed Oil** (recommended)
   - Food-safe
   - Enhances grain
   - Easy application
   - Natural look

2. **Tung Oil**
   - Water-resistant
   - Durable
   - Slightly darker tone

3. **Mineral Oil**
   - Food-safe
   - No odor
   - Easy maintenance

**Application Method:**
1. Apply oil liberally with cloth
2. Let soak for 10-15 minutes
3. Wipe off excess
4. Let cure 24-48 hours
5. Optional: Second coat for richer finish

## 🔤 Engraving Specifications

### Method: CNC Engraving

**Depth:** 0.02-0.05" (0.5-1.3 mm) - deeper than laser for better visibility  
**Bit Type:** V-bit (60° or 90°) for text, ball-nose for decorative elements  
**Location:** Center of lid top panel  
**Area:** Approximately 6" x 2" rectangle  
**Time:** Approximately 5 minutes per box  
**Setup:** Requires fixture to hold lid securely during engraving

### Text Layout

**Default Layout:**
```
        [Couple Names - Optional]
    Our Forever Starts Here
        EST. [Date]
```

**Font Specifications:**
- **Style:** Elegant script font
- **Main Text Size:** 0.5" height (12.7 mm)
- **Date Size:** 0.3" height (7.6 mm)
- **Line Spacing:** Appropriate for readability

**Personalization Options:**
1. **Wedding Version:**
   - Text: "Our Forever Starts Here"
   - Date: "EST. [Wedding Date]" (e.g., "EST. OCT 26, 2024")
   - Optional: Couple names above text

2. **Anniversary Version:**
   - Text: "Established [Date]"
   - Optional: Couple names
   - Optional: Years together

3. **Generic Version:**
   - Text: Custom message (customer provided)
   - Optional: Date
   - Optional: Names

## 🔗 Joinery Specifications

### Primary Method: Box Joints (Finger Joints)

**Joint Type:** Box/Finger Joints  
**Finger Size:** 0.5" (12.7 mm) - matches material thickness  
**Finger Count:** 4-6 fingers per joint  
**Tolerance:** Tight fit, minimal gaps (<0.005")

**Advantages:**
- Strong and durable
- Easier to manufacture than dovetails
- Can be CNC-cut for consistency
- Good for production efficiency
- Visible joints add rustic character

**Alternative Method: Dovetail Joints**
- More traditional, premium look
- Very strong
- More time-consuming
- Consider for premium version
