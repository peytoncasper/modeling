## ⚠️ Critical Finding: COGS vs Market Price

**Problem:** Our calculated COGS ($98.68) is **higher** than the market leader's price ($89.95).

**Analysis:**
- Market leader sells at $89.95 with 538 sales/month
- Our COGS is $98.68 (9.7% higher than market price)
- We cannot compete at $89.95 without losing money

**Possible Explanations:**
1. **Bulk Material Pricing:** Market leader likely gets better bulk pricing
2. **Efficiency:** They may have optimized production (batch processing)
3. **Labor Costs:** They may have lower labor costs or automation
4. **Material Substitution:** They may use cheaper materials (different wood, simpler hardware)
5. **Volume Discounts:** Higher volume = better supplier pricing

## 🔧 Cost Reduction Strategies

### Option 1: Optimize Material Costs

**Current Material Cost:** $16.90  
**Target:** $12-14 (reduce by 20-30%)

**Strategies:**
- Source oak at bulk pricing ($4-5/bf instead of $6/bf)
- Use simpler hardware (magnetic closure instead of latch/lock)
- Reduce fabric cost (use felt instead of velvet)
- Optimize wood usage (reduce waste)

**Potential Savings:** $3-5 per box

### Option 2: Increase Production Efficiency

**Current Labor:** 2.5 hours  
**Target:** 1.5-2 hours (reduce by 20-40%)

**Strategies:**
- Batch production (5-10 boxes at once)
- CNC optimization (faster cutting/engraving)
- Streamlined assembly process
- Reduce sanding time (better initial cuts)

**Potential Savings:** $10-20 per box

### Option 3: Target Premium Segment

**Strategy:** Position as premium product at $120-140

**Justification:**
- Higher quality materials (premium oak, better hardware)
- Better craftsmanship
- More detailed engraving
- Superior finish

**Market Validation:**

- HighPointGifts sells at $279 with 10 sales/month
- Premium segment exists but smaller volume
