## 🎨 Design Style Guidelines

### Rustic Farmhouse Style
- **Wood:** Reclaimed oak, barn wood, weathered textures
- **Hardware:** Antique brass, vintage locks
- **Fonts:** Script, handwritten, rustic serif
- **Colors:** Natural wood tones, cream, warm whites
- **Details:** Hand-carved, weathered, natural grain visible

### Modern Minimalist Style
- **Wood:** Walnut, birch, smooth finishes
- **Hardware:** Magnetic closures, sleek hardware
- **Fonts:** Sans-serif, geometric, clean lines
- **Colors:** Natural wood, gray, white, black
- **Details:** Laser-engraved, clean lines, geometric patterns

### Vintage Heirloom Style
- **Wood:** Cherry, mahogany, rich tones
- **Hardware:** Antique brass, ornate details
- **Fonts:** Serif, classic, traditional
- **Colors:** Rich wood tones, burgundy, gold accents
- **Details:** Hand-carved, ornate, traditional motifs

### Whimsical Nursery Style
- **Wood:** Maple, pine, light tones
- **Hardware:** Heart-shaped locks, playful elements
- **Fonts:** Playful script, bubble letters, fun fonts
- **Colors:** Pastels, bright colors, soft tones
- **Details:** Animal motifs, playful patterns, rounded edges

---

## 📸 Photography Style Guidelines

### Product Photography Requirements
- **Lighting:** Soft, natural, professional studio lighting
- **Background:** White, neutral gray, or soft pastels
- **Depth of Field:** Shallow for product focus
- **Angles:** Multiple angles (front, side, interior, detail shots)
- **Style:** Clean, professional, Etsy-ready

### Mood & Atmosphere
- **Wedding/Anniversary:** Romantic, warm, elegant
- **Memorial:** Respectful, dignified, emotional
- **Baby/Nursery:** Cheerful, bright, family-friendly

---

## 🎯 Prompt Structure Template

For each product variation, include:
1. **Product Type:** Specific product name
2. **Style:** Design aesthetic (rustic, modern, vintage, whimsical)
3. **Wood Type:** Specific wood species
4. **Dimensions:** Size specifications
5. **Details:** Engraving, hardware, interior lining
6. **Personalization:** Space for names, dates, messages
7. **Photography:** Lighting, background, style
8. **Mood:** Emotional tone and aesthetic

## 💡 Usage Instructions

### For Image Generation Models:
1. Use prompts as-is for initial concept generation
2. Generate 3-4 variations per product
3. Select best designs for prototyping
4. Refine based on manufacturing feasibility
5. Create final product photos using actual products

### For Design Iteration:
1. Generate multiple variations
2. Mix and match elements (e.g., rustic wood + modern font)
3. Test different personalization options
4. Consider customer preferences from research
5. Optimize for manufacturing efficiency
