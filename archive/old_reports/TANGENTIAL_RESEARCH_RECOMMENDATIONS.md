**Date:** 2024-12-31

---

## 🎯 Executive Summary

After analyzing **wedding products** (25+ validated), **gaming accessories** (19 validated), and **broad product research** (23 categories), I've identified **3 tangential areas** that offer the highest potential based on:

1. **Natural lifecycle progression** from proven markets
2. **Similar emotional value** to high-performing products
3. **High gift-giving potential**
4. **Wood-based product fit** (your strength)
5. **Repeat customer potential**
