## 🎯 Key Insights

### 1. Order Volume is Manageable

**77-100 orders/month = 2.6-3.3 orders/day**

This is **very achievable** for a solo operation or small team:
- Can handle 3-4 orders/day comfortably
- Allows time for quality work
- Not overwhelming

---

### 2. Price Point Matters

**Higher prices = Fewer orders needed**

- $100 avg = 100 orders/month
- $130 avg = 77 orders/month (23% fewer!)

**Strategy:** Focus on premium positioning to reduce order volume

---

### 3. Product Mix Helps

**Diversification reduces risk:**
- Not dependent on one product
- Different price points
- Different customer segments
- Seasonal variations balanced

---

### 4. Production Efficiency

**At 77-100 orders/month:**
- Batch production becomes efficient
- Can optimize workflows
- Reduce per-unit costs
- Scale more easily

---

## 💡 Optimization Strategies

### 1. Increase Average Order Value

**Tactics:**
- Offer upsells (personalization, premium materials)
- Create bundles (Memory Box + Photo Frame)
- Higher-priced variations (larger sizes)
- Add-on products (engraving, gift boxes)

**Impact:** Increase AOV from $130 to $150 = 15% fewer orders needed

---

### 2. Focus on High-Margin Products

**Prioritize:**
- Memorial Display Box ($86 avg, premium pricing)
- Memory/Keepsake Box ($130 avg, high value)
- Baby Growth Chart ($74 avg, but high volume)

**Impact:** Better margins = more profit per order

---

### 3. Batch Production

**Efficiency Gains:**
- Cut multiple pieces at once
- Sand/finish in batches
- Engrave multiple items
- Package efficiently

**Impact:** Reduce production time by 30-40%

---

### 4. Streamline Fulfillment

**Optimizations:**
- Pre-cut common sizes
- Pre-finish standard options
- Template-based personalization
- Efficient packaging system

**Impact:** Reduce fulfillment time by 20-30%
